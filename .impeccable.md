# Sombra design context

## Design Context

### Users
Crypto-native users on Solana who care about privacy — traders, founders, power users who are already conscious of on-chain surveillance (front-running bots, chain analytics, traffic harvesting by future quantum adversaries). They arrive at the site from Twitter, developer newsletters, or founder channels, primarily on desktop but increasingly on mobile. They are evaluating whether Sombra is serious, credible, and worth trusting with real positions.

### Brand Personality
Voice: **cinematic, confident, technical but refined** (three words).

The copy leans editorial — "Trade in the shadow.", "Your positions, unseen.", "Harvest now, decrypt later." Tone is declarative, not promotional. Technical specificity (ML-KEM, ML-DSA, CRYSTALS-Kyber) sits next to serif statements — a magazine cover for cryptographers.

Emotional goal: the user should feel that Sombra is both *beautiful* and *serious*. Not a flashy crypto marketing site; a considered product built by people who respect cryptographic correctness.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial/cinematic, with a slight technical undertone. Three themes coexist:
- **Eclipse** (default, dark): warm amber accent on near-black. Cinematic.
- **Dusk** (warm light): dark brown on cream. Editorial magazine.
- **Grid** (brutal light): burnt red on off-white. Technical/brutalist.

The **eclipse mark** (dark disc + amber corona) is the identity anchor. The Instrument Serif italic is the voice. Mono (JetBrains) is used sparingly for labels and technical callouts.

Anti-references:
- Generic crypto sites (cyan-on-dark, purple→blue gradients, neon everything) — explicitly forbidden.
- "Enterprise SaaS" safeness (rounded rectangles, drop shadows, pastel gradients).
- Hype-heavy wallet marketing.

### Design Principles
1. **The eclipse is the mark.** The dark-center-with-amber-corona anchors identity. Reuse it, don't multiply marks.
2. **Editorial type hierarchy.** Instrument Serif italic carries emotion; a refined sans carries body; mono carries technical truth. Big contrast between display and body sizes (≥1.3 ratio).
3. **Warm, not cool.** Amber and burnt tones over blues and teals. Neutrals tint toward the brand hue.
4. **Confident negative space.** Large empty fields are intentional, not wasted. Don't fill every area with a card.
5. **Motion is rare but purposeful.** Pillar visuals share a 2-second beat. The eclipse breathes. Everything else is still. Reduce or disable motion on mobile where it fights momentum scroll.
6. **Mobile is not a shrunk desktop.** Rearrange structure; don't just compress. The eclipse moves above the text; the pillars stack; the nav links become an icon stack.

### Accessibility
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere (already baked into the pillar visuals, lattice echo, and shielded-button glow).
- Color contrast must hold on all three themes — dim text is not muted to the point of invisibility.
- Theme-adaptive tokens (`--ink`, `--accent`, etc.) so no hardcoded colors appear outside the icon/favicon.
