# Sombra design context

Source of truth: Figma "Sombra – Product Design" (Website page; brand rules on "Internal Brand Guidelines").

### Users
Organizations and crypto-native users on Solana who need confidentiality — treasuries, payroll, B2B settlement, traders — and the security and compliance teams who vet them. They need Sombra to read as serious, auditable infrastructure, not a mixer.

### Brand Personality
"Sombra as protective shade." Calm, assured, foundational — a constant. Nature's enduring strength: stability, protection, resilience. Copy is short and declarative ("Your wallet. Your business.", "Three steps. One chain. Zero bridges.").

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light pages, generous whitespace, full-bleed nature photography (canyon, rock, hills, grass, coast, mountains) carrying quiet statements.
- One typeface: **APK Galeria** (single weight, no italic) for everything. Headings ~48px, tight tracking (-0.05em); body 16–20px.
- Palette: paper `#fff`, surface `#f8f7f6`, ink `#1a1a1a`, muted `#636762`, line `#d6d9d3`, pine `#293935` (primary actions, dark bands), canyon `#a73d13` (rare emphasis — "Book demo"). Tokens live in `app/globals.css`.
- Product UI shown as glass cards over photos; diagrams as calm line art.
- Page content ends in a 48px rounded edge over the ink footer with its giant "sombra" wordmark.

Anti-references: neon crypto gradients, dark hype aesthetics, decorative motion.

### Design Principles
1. **Shade, not shadow.** Protective and warm, never ominous.
2. **One voice in type.** APK Galeria only; hierarchy through size and tracking, not weight or italics.
3. **Photography carries emotion; UI carries proof.** Statements over photos; product cards and diagrams show how it works.
4. **Motion explains.** The value-flow loop and step accordions teach the mechanism; everything else stays still.
5. **Mobile is designed, not shrunk.** Every section has its own 402px layout in Figma.

### Accessibility
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (value flow, ring animation, values slider all hold a static frame).
- Check contrast of text over photos (overlays are part of the design).
- Use tokens, not hardcoded colors, outside exported artwork.
